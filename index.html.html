The problem

Fixed-time signals don’t react to how many vehicles are actually waiting. The result is wasted time and fuel, idling exhaust and daily stress for commuters.

How it works

From sensing to a decision commuters can see.

Collect vehicle counts and air-quality readings from sensors or simulated inputs.
Process readings with timestamps and locations to estimate density.
Analyze congestion and calculate signal-timing suggestions within safety limits.
Display traffic status, pollution and route suggestions on a dashboard.
Evaluate in simulation against fixed-time signals, then improve.

Where we are

Prototype concept. We are building a digital prototype with simulated data. Live sensing, real signal control and live routing are future goals that need testing, safety validation and authorization. We’re looking for commuter feedback and pilot partners.
